MENELAO

Redundant copy · SHA-256 · verification · reports

Local desktop application that copies one source to two independent destinations and verifies the X/A/B triangle before the operator reuses the original media. The design prioritizes data integrity, non-destructive operation and verifiable evidence.

Problem
A completed copy does not prove by itself that the data reached both destinations intact.
Approach
SHA-256 hashes, X/A/B comparison, manifests, reports and an explicitly non-destructive workflow.
Stack
Python · Tkinter · pytest · SHA-256 · Linux.
